compare_candidates v6.0.0
Compare identity and nonidentity candidates only through a validated r22-structured-evidence-v1 report. A nonidentity candidate must appear in the deterministic admitted set and have complete operation effects, four horizon buckets, paired support, similarity-weighted evidence, uncertainty, leave-one-out stability, downside, calibration and width deltas, operation-specific state contrast, and nonzero current disagreement. Lower QS is preferred, but historical gain alone never proves current accuracy. Do not recompute evidence, inspect raw episodes or targets, relax frozen thresholds, restore rejected candidates, or use free-form rationale. If report fields disagree, are incomplete, nonfinite, stale, or provenance-invalid, prefer identity.
