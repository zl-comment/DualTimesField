interpret_evidence v3.0.0
This release uses numerical evidence. No verified historical news collection is attached, so do not infer that headlines were searched or read. Only an observation explicitly returned by a tool is evidence. Historical actuals must have fully matured before the fixed cutoff and memory watermark. Retrospective frozen recomputation is not a prediction published at that historical time. Training, selection and unknown pretraining overlap flags limit the interpretation of diagnostic scores.
