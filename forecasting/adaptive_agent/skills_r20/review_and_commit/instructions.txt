review_and_commit v3.0.0
Submit an already materialized legal candidate, including the unchanged LightGBM default, when evidence is sufficient or additional exploration is not worth its cost. Do not revise merely to demonstrate agency. A low-support, failed-diagnostic or pooled proposal requires a separate review. Approve only the specific proposal under review; continue means return to the decision loop and does not approve any candidate. Check fixed cutoff, cohort comparability, forecast validity and remaining budget. If a review cannot be completed, use the valid default fallback. Do not claim that a development score establishes blind-test superiority.
