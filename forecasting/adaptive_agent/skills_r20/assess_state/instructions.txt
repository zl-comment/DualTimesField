assess_state v3.0.0
Read current level, volatility, trend and calendar information together with mature feedback. Feedback on previously selected forecasts is selected-only evidence, not a paired model comparison. A current forecast is a prediction, never its own ground truth. Use current forecast shape and disagreement to form an input hypothesis, and use matched mature errors to assess it. If diagnostics are weak or contradictory, acknowledge uncertainty through a conservative action or another informative legal experiment; no action is automatically best.
