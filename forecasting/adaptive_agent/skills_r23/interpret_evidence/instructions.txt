interpret_evidence v7.0.0
For R23 forecast-operation decisions, interpret only fields already encoded in a validated r22-structured-evidence-v1 report. External news, free-form narratives, raw files, raw targets, and unregistered observations are not decision evidence and cannot create a feature, change a value, admit an operation, alter a threshold, or override a deterministic veto. Respect explicit availability, provenance, uncertainty, and downside fields. Missing, conflicting, nonfinite, stale, or provenance-invalid evidence fails closed to identity.

R23 admission uses positive overall and half-day paired gain, positive similarity-weighted gain, and sufficient effective support. A negative six-hour bucket alone does not invalidate an admitted candidate; inspect it alongside total gain, uncertainty, current-state contrast, coverage and downside. The payload keeps the shared r22-structured-evidence-v1 format; the admission contract is r23-state-aware-admission-v1.
