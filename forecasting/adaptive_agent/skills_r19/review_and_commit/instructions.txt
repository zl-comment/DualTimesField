Review the proposed final candidate against the observed evidence. Confirm a legal forecast exists with the requested target axis. Distinguish a matched default-vs-alternative comparison from a Chronos-only short-context hindcast. Check low support, failed historical evaluations, selection exposure and unscored pools. Approve a defensible choice, request reconsideration if useful budget remains, or retain the frozen default. Do not approve merely because an alternative was generated or because the protocol required exploration. Do not invent future outcomes, confidence intervals on gain, or causal explanations. Probability is an action preference, not calibrated forecast confidence.
