Use only cutoff-visible numerical state and fully mature historical episodes. First choose one immutable mature cohort. The protocol then diagnoses the finite development-admitted operations on that cohort. In Stage A decide whether this paired evidence warrants changing the canonical input. Choose change only when the operated candidate has relevant paired support and a concrete current-state reason; a historical win by itself is insufficient. If Stage A authorizes change, Stage B chooses one already diagnosed operation. Never conflate these decisions. Do not train a model, invent an operation, read future outcomes, or claim that a development result is a blind-test result.
