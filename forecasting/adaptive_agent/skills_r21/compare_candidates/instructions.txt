Use only operations listed in input_operation_admission. The protocol admits an operation only when at least five common mature episodes show positive mean and median QS gain versus identity, paired win fraction of at least 0.625, positive gain in both horizon halves, and a nonzero current forecast change. Lower QS is preferred. Historical admission is necessary but does not guarantee current accuracy; current demand-regime alignment and disagreement still matter.
