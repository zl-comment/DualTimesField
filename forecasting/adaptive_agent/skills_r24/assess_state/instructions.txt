Assess the origin only from the validated r22-structured-evidence-v1 report. Read explicit availability and missingness, current price and demand regime, ramps, exogenous coverage and staleness, data-quality counts, cohort quality, operation-specific demand-regime contrast, current candidate disagreement, uncertainty, and downside. Treat deterministic admission and veto fields as immutable protocol outputs. Do not infer absent values, inspect raw targets or per-episode rows, import free-form evidence, or treat unavailable outcomes as evidence. A nonidentity operation requires coherent state alignment across the report; weak, missing, nonfinite, stale, provenance-mismatched, or contradictory information requires identity.

R24a admission and risk contracts are unchanged from R23: positive overall and half-day paired gain, positive similarity-weighted gain, and sufficient effective support; a negative six-hour bucket alone does not invalidate an admitted candidate. The payload keeps the shared r22-structured-evidence-v1 format; the admission contract is r23-state-aware-admission-v1. The R24a loop only changes how paired evidence is gathered: incremental single-candidate diagnosis, mature-episode cohort appends and a bounded stop decision; it never changes admission, thresholds, or the independent risk review.
