review_and_commit v8.1.0
Stage C is a separate risk-only review of one already selected nonidentity candidate. Use only the selected candidate, identity, the validated r22-structured-evidence-v1 report, current forecast comparison, and deterministic veto results; do not use Stage A or Stage B rationale. Return exactly approve_selected or revert_to_identity. approve_selected commits the unchanged selected candidate. revert_to_identity commits identity with a registered reason code. A hard veto, missing or conflicting field, nonfinite value, provenance mismatch, malformed input, insufficient support, data-quality failure, budget failure, timeout, exception, or unavailable review requires revert_to_identity. Never switch to another operation, invent or modify a candidate, request new evidence, rerun retrieval, alter thresholds, override a veto, retrain, or change model inputs. Identity bypasses Stage C and commits directly. Stage C never sees loop, Stage A or Stage B rationale.

R24a admission and risk contracts are unchanged from R23: positive overall and half-day paired gain, positive similarity-weighted gain, and sufficient effective support; a negative six-hour bucket alone does not invalidate an admitted candidate. The payload keeps the shared r22-structured-evidence-v1 format; the admission contract is r23-state-aware-admission-v1. The R24a loop only changes how paired evidence is gathered: incremental single-candidate diagnosis, mature-episode cohort appends and a bounded stop decision; it never changes admission, thresholds, or the independent risk review. A cross-time candidate ledger is additionally visible: every materialized candidate of earlier origins in this arm, fully matured at +86400, aggregated per candidate with mean QS/MAE, the difference against the identity candidate mean, and demand-regime state-bucket support counts. The ledger is within-arm evidence, not a paired comparison and not a random sample; sparse support counts mean weak evidence. Use it to judge whether the current state bucket matches the buckets where an operation historically helped; it never overrides deterministic admission or vetoes.
