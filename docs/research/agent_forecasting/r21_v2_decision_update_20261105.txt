Decision protocol

For every origin, the protocol still locks one mature historical cohort and
recomputes identity plus every registered operation with the same frozen
LightGBM. It now computes per-episode QS gains against identity. An operation
is exposed to Kev only if all of these fixed checks pass:

1. at least five common fully mature episodes;
2. positive mean identity-minus-operation QS gain;
3. positive median identity-minus-operation QS gain;
4. paired win fraction at least 0.625;
5. positive QS gain in both the first and last 12 forecast hours;
6. nonzero current forecast disagreement from identity.

If no operation passes, the protocol commits identity without asking Kev to
override the result. If one or more pass, Kev Stage A may still choose identity
after reviewing the current numerical state. Stage B can name only an admitted
operation. The decision record also supplies a registered demand-regime
contrast: latest 24 hours versus the older 48 hours for the 24-hour profile,
or recent 48 hours versus the oldest 24 hours for the 48-hour profile.

Claim boundary

The thresholds are transparent engineering guardrails. They are not a trained
classifier and do not establish causal benefit. The 2022H2 calibration origins
were already used by R21 v1, so any v2 result on them is iterative development,
not an unbiased validation result. The 2023-2024 formal split has prior project
exposure and cannot support an independent-blind claim. The two operations
remain training-normalization-mean counterfactuals, not missing-value masks or
literal column deletion.
