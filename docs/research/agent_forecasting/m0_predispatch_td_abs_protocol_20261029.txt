3. Receipt visibility
- The only visibility clock is receipt_complete_unix: the local UTC wall clock
  read in the same process at stream EOF, ceiled to the next whole second (the
  recorded instant is never earlier than the true EOF), stored beside a
  monotonic reading and a clock-health record (wall/monotonic advances).
- Raw bodies are stored write-once under bodies/<sha256>.zip; receipt events are
  appended to events.jsonl.  load_receipt_store re-reads and re-hashes every
  body and re-parses it through receipt_from_raw_body; any drift fails closed.
- HTTP Date/Last-Modified, Content-Length, LASTCHANGED, filename time and
  directory ordering never grant visibility.

4. Revision, duplicates, conflicts
- Same (seqno, runno, body_sha256) received again: all receipt events are kept,
  the signal folds to the EARLIEST body-complete instant.
- Same (seqno, runno) with two different body hashes: fork, fail closed, no
  last-write-wins.
- Header/column/version drift, a second REGION_SOLUTION section, orphan D rows,
  duplicate primary keys, empty or non-finite TOTALDEMAND, off-grid DATETIME,
  wrong trailer count: rejected, no repair, no migration.
- Caller-supplied rows, values, body hashes or basis strings can never build or
  promote a receipt: construction exists only via receipt_from_raw_body
  (first_seen_receipt) and synthetic_receipt_from_raw_body (synthetic_fixture);
  both re-parse the raw bytes on every (re)construction; dataclasses.replace
  cannot flip basis, rows or hashes.

5. Target-time mapping and no-fill
- All AEMO times are AEST (UTC+10, no DST).  Target hour h in [0,24) is the
  delivery hour [origin+h, origin+(h+1)) (origin must be hour-aligned UTC).
- The value for hour h is the exact arithmetic mean of the two half-hour rows
  ending at origin+(2h+1)*1800 and origin+(2h+2)*1800, taken from ONE vintage.
- Selection: among deduplicated receipts visible at origin (receipt_complete <=
  origin), only the NEWEST (seqno, runno) is examined.  If it does not carry all
  48 exact half-hour ends, the origin is UNCOVERED: no fallback to older or
  longer runs, no archive, no cross-vintage splice, no forward/back/median/zero
  fill of any kind.
- Rows outside the 48-end window are ignored (never spliced in).

6. Typed input contract extension
- FrozenForecastInput gains a future_forecasts mapping allowlisted to
  predispatchis_totaldemand_mw, length 24, finite MW, bound in metadata to
  source_manifest_sha256 = series_hash (origin, values, vintage identity, body
  and header hashes, ledger hash).
- The typed field is exempt from the calendar rule "future covariates need
  same-named past covariates".  Inventing a 72h past for this series is
  forbidden; the name cannot appear in past_covariates at all.
- Tool encoding (frozen): Chronos-2 (chronos-forecasting 2.3.2) rejects a
  future covariate whose name has no past stream (validated empirically:
  from_list_of_dicts raises "must be a subset of past_covariates"), and masks
  NaN context entries natively (model.py _prepare_patched_context /
  _prepare_patched_future).  frozen_chronos.build_model_input therefore encodes
  the honest absence as an all-NaN 72-step past for the typed name and passes
  the 24 real future values through unchanged.  No past value is invented; the
  response check records this encoding in every trace.
