  \caption{Detected-event dual-field forecaster. Events detected in the
    72-hour history, as the largest departures from the window median after
    non-maximum suppression, form the discrete event field (DGF); the
    continuous trend field (CTF) fits the remainder under the decomposition
    loss. Field-specific linear heads read field-matched AEMO forecasts (gas
    price and net load for the CTF, spare capacity and shortfalls for the DGF)
    and are fused by a trigonometric gate with per-quantile variants.}
