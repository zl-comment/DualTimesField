  \caption{Dual-field forecaster with a linear base. Events detected in the
    72-hour history, as the largest departures from the window median after
    non-maximum suppression, form the discrete event field (DGF); the
    continuous trend field (CTF) fits the remainder under the decomposition
    loss. Field-specific linear heads and a linear base on the raw history
    and all known inputs (AEMO predispatch, PD PASA spare capacity and net
    load, gas price, calendar) are fused by a trigonometric gate; a per-hour
    calibrator corrects the response to the known inputs, and the forecast is
    clipped at a price floor chosen on the validation quarter. Every model is
    refit before each test quarter.}
  \label{fig:architecture}
\end{figure*}
